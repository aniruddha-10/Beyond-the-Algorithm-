Findings · the answer to our proposal's question

How personalized are the ads we actually see?

Not very. Most ads felt generic, Instagram felt more personal than YouTube without targeting us any better, and who was scrolling mattered more than which app.

Takeaway 1

7 in 10 ads didn't feel personal at all.

  • 46%missed us completely rated 1–2
  • 26%were of general interest rated 3
  • 17%felt genuinely useful rated 4–5
  • 11%felt too personal felt intrusive

Every ad counted once. Counting each person equally: 49% · 24% · 16% · 11%.

Takeaway 2 · our four dimensions

Instagram felt more personal, without targeting us better.

  • TargetingEvenmatched our interests · IG 53.4% · YT 53.4%
  • RelevanceInstagramaverage rating · IG 2.64 · YT 2.37
  • EngagementInstagram, 3×clicked · IG 11.1% · YT 3.4%
  • PrivacyInstagramfelt too personal · IG 13.5% · YT 8.1%

Counting each person equally gives the same verdicts.

Takeaway 3

Who was scrolling mattered more than which app.

26% to 69%

How often an ad missed ranged from 26% for P1 to 69% for P5. Between the two apps it differed by just 8 points (42% Instagram, 50% YouTube).

One person's YouTube felt useful 57% of the time (P2); another's, never (P4).
